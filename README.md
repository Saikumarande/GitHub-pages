# Cron-expression-answers
## Answers to the Cron Expression Quiz

1. **Expression:** `0 0 6 1 * ?`
   - **Answer:** Fires at 6:00 AM on the 1st day of every month.

2. **Expression:** `0 30 9 ? * 2#2`
   - **Answer:** Fires at 9:30 AM on the second Tuesday of every month.

3. **Expression:** `0 15 14 15 * ?`
   - **Answer:** Fires at 2:15 PM on the 15th day of every month.

4. **Expression:** `0 0/10 8-10 * * ?`
   - **Answer:** Fires every 10 minutes between 8:00 AM and 10:59 AM every day.

5. **Expression:** `0 0 12 ? * 5L`
   - **Answer:** Fires at 12:00 PM on the last Thursday of every month.

6. **Expression:** `0 45 17 ? * MON-FRI`
   - **Answer:** Fires at 5:45 PM every weekday (Monday to Friday).

7. **Expression:** `0 0 7 1/3 * ?`
   - **Answer:** Fires at 7:00 AM every 3 days starting from the 1st day of the month.

8. **Expression:** `0 0 18 1,15 * ?`
   - **Answer:** Fires at 6:00 PM on the 1st and 15th day of every month.

9. **Expression:** `0 30 8 ? * 6#1`
   - **Answer:** Fires at 8:30 AM on the first Friday of every month.

10. **Expression:** `0 0 0 1 1 ?`
    - **Answer:** Fires at midnight (12:00 AM) on January 1st every year.
